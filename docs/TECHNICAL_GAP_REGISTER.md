## Priority Classification

- **P0 (Safety & Security Blockers):** Must be closed and verified before any real patient data, external pilot, or live dispensing.
- **P1 (Pilot-Critical Traceability & Patient Support):** Required before controlled institutional pilot (Gate G2 & G3).
- **P2 (Stewardship Intelligence, Governance & AI Assistance):** Required for clinical stewardship adoption and governed AI evaluation.
- **P3 (Institutional & National Scale):** Required for multi-facility / national scale operations.
